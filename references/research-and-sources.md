# Research and regional adaptation

Start with a written local evidence plan. Sources inform the screen; no source provider endorses this composite model.

| Research need | Reusable method | Regional inputs |
|---|---|---|
| Geography | Match properties to actual boundaries, preserve overlaps and exceptions | Municipal/county GIS, neighborhood definitions and address/parcel records |
| Buyer depth | Review recurring comparable demand and plausible landlord/household exits | Deduplicated closed sales and supported local rental evidence |
| Liquidity | Preserve duration, price-reduction and listing-event definitions | Coherent local sales and listing snapshots for a defined housing product |
| Trajectory | Compare like periods and compatible products | Local vacancy/condition records, market series, operating services and completed improvements |
| As-is value | Reconcile competitive closed sales with subject differences | Sale conditions, product/condition/size/location fit and current entry terms |
| Renovation | Distinguish repeatable work from established exceptions | Scoped local quotes, permits, completed-project evidence and access assumptions |
| Resilience | Attach actual physical, use, financing and insurance issues to the affected property | Effective hazard maps, local code/use records and actual transaction constraints |

Keep observations, assumptions and unresolved issues distinguishable. Do not average incompatible geographies or products into a precise-looking value, infer buyer identity from a company name, treat gross rent as net income, or call a mixed-sales median a like-for-like appreciation rate. Missing local data supports limited conviction, not an invented measured outcome.

For comparison principles, consult [Fannie Mae comparable selection](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.3-08/comparable-sales) and [market-supported adjustments](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b4-1.3-09/adjustments-comparable-sales). These inform research judgment; this screen does not claim compliance with formal appraisal requirements.

For a United States application, possible starting points include [ACS geographic guidance](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/guidance/estimates.html), [Redfin metric methodology](https://www.redfin.com/news/data-center/methodology/) and the [FEMA Map Service Center](https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home). Preserve geography, dates, margins of error and metric definitions. Other countries require their own sources and legal/transaction assumptions.

Read [the Albany profile](../regions/albany-ny.md) only for an Albany application. Its particular sources, boundaries and conclusions do not transfer automatically to another market. Do not publish raw restricted records, assignment files or bulk vendor exports as part of a sharing package.
