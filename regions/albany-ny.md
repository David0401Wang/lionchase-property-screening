# Albany, New York — regional application profile

This profile documents local choices in the original 40-property coursework application. It is context for research, not a current listing feed or a universal geographic configuration. The public example is fictional; the actual 40-property dataset and team documents are excluded from this repository.

## Geographic scope

The working set covered 39 properties in the City of Albany and one in the Town of Colonie/Roessleville area. An Albany mailing address did not establish City of Albany membership. Some municipal neighborhood polygons overlapped, so combined labels were retained rather than creating duplicate property rows.

For a fresh Albany analysis, verify the current [city neighborhood directory](https://albanyny.gov/689/Albany-Neighborhoods) and [public map](https://albany-ny.tolemi.com/), together with county/state address or parcel records and jurisdiction boundaries. Provider labels and official polygons may differ. Do not treat a previously matched address as a current permit, occupancy or legal-use certification.

## Local research choices

- Distinguish single-family, two-family, condo and mixed-use products; they have different buyers, financing constraints and comparable sets.
- Use competitive closed-sale evidence within a coherent period. Adjacent Menands, Colonie or other markets need explicit boundary and product caveats.
- Retain source-specific dates, mixed-product market scope, listing continuity limits and cached-status conflicts.
- Consult the [Albany Citizen's Self-Service Portal](https://www.albanyny.gov/2156/Citizens-Self-Service-Portal) for relevant use, permit and code research. Tax assessment classes alone do not certify lawful occupancy or rentable unit counts.
- Keep cash-only terms, condo warrantability, occupancy, moisture damage and utility shutoffs attached to the specific affected property. Unknown condition or building age alone is not a confirmed defect.
- Estimate renovation, taxes, transaction/holding costs and timing locally. No universal repair cost per square foot, property appreciation rate or rental yield is encoded here.

## What to replace for another region

Replace the actual property list, jurisdiction and boundary checks, comparable transactions, source/metric definitions, local legal and financing research, work-cost assumptions, observation dates and target product cohorts. Review whether the fixed model anchors fit the new mandate before applying them consistently. Do not transfer Albany prices, ratings or investment conclusions.

The reusable code requires no Albany polygon files or personal computer paths. It operates on researched JSON inputs. Regional adaptation remains analyst/agent work and has not been empirically validated across cities.
